# Community Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, accessible community for people of every background and level of legal knowledge.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Be kind, patient, constructive, and respectful.
- Explain ideas without shaming people for not knowing something.
- Welcome different experiences, languages, abilities, and learning styles.
- Give feedback about work, not personal worth.
- Respect privacy and do not post another person's personal or study information.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, intimidation, sexual harassment, and repeated hostile or insulting conduct.

## Reporting

Please report harmful behaviour privately to the project maintainers using the contact method published in the repository's GitHub profile or project page. Do not post sensitive details publicly. Maintainers will review reports fairly and may edit or remove content, issue warnings, or exclude contributors when necessary.

This code of conduct applies to project spaces, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, and events run by the project.
