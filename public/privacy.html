Your study data stays in your browser.

This notice explains how the current LawLearn Commons beta handles information. It is an early-stage project notice, not a substitute for a legal review of the project's privacy obligations.

What the app stores

The app stores subjects/modules, personal notes, study tasks and saved revision cards in the browser's local storage on the device you are using. The project team does not receive this study data through the app. It is not synchronised to another device or browser.

AI and uploads

AI is disabled in this beta. The app does not send questions or study material to Claude, Gemini, Together AI, OpenAI or another model provider. Plain-text files you choose to load are read locally by your browser. There is no PDF extraction service.

Accounts and analytics

This beta has no student accounts, login, email collection, central study database, advertising trackers or analytics scripts.

Hosting and external links

The hosting provider may process basic technical connection information such as IP address, request time and browser/device information under its own terms. When you open a third-party legal resource, that site receives your request and its own privacy terms apply.

Your choices

  • Use the Export data control to download a JSON backup.
  • Use Clear my local data to remove LawLearn study data from this browser.
  • Clearing browser/site data may also remove your saved study data.
  • Avoid shared devices and do not store confidential, privileged or sensitive information in the beta.

Legal accuracy

LawLearn Commons is an educational tool, not legal advice or an official legal database. The local summary, flashcard and quiz tools can simplify or omit context. Check original legal sources and prescribed course materials.

Changes

This notice applies to the current no-account, no-AI public beta. A future version with accounts, cloud storage, AI providers, or feedback forms will require an updated privacy notice and appropriate consent and security controls before those features are enabled.
