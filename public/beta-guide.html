Welcome, tester.

Thank you for helping us make legal learning clearer and more accessible. This is an early test version, not a finished legal research product.

What you can test

  1. Add a subject: set up the modules you are studying.
  2. Quick summary: paste your own notes or load a small text file. The tool selects key sentences; it does not verify the law.
  3. Case brief: fill in the facts, issue, outcome, reasoning and principle from a judgment you are studying.
  4. Flashcards and quiz: generate simple recall prompts from your notes. Check them yourself.
  5. Notebook and planner: save notes and deadlines, then export a backup.
  6. Legal resources: open the source links and check the original documents and their dates.

What is not enabled

AI is deliberately switched off. There is no legal database search, AI case analysis, account system, central storage, automatic PDF reading, or cross-device sync in this beta.

How your information is handled

Your notes and study data are stored in your current browser on your current device. The project does not collect your name, email, student number, or study notes in a central database. The hosting provider may process basic technical request information. Read the privacy notice.

Important safety rules

  • Do not enter client information, privileged/confidential material, passwords, ID numbers or sensitive personal data.
  • Do not use a shared computer unless you are comfortable with your browser retaining local study data; clear it when finished.
  • Export a backup before changing browsers/devices or clearing browser data.
  • Check every legal statement against your prescribed materials and original legal sources.
  • Tell us about mistakes, confusing screens and accessibility problems before relying on the tool for exams.

Five-minute feedback

Try at least one summary, one case brief, one flashcard deck and one planner task. Note what worked, what confused you, and what feature would help most. The beta does not yet include a feedback submission form; feedback can be sent directly to the person who invited you.
