# South African legal information resources

The current beta only links to external resources. It does not crawl, copy, index, embed or republish their content. Check source dates and terms directly before relying on or reusing a document.

| Resource | What it can help with | Important limitation |
|---|---|---|
| [Department of Justice — Constitution](https://www.justice.gov.za/legislation/constitution/) | Constitution text, amendments and related resources | Confirm the relevant text and amendment status for the question. |
| [South African Government — Constitution](https://www.gov.za/documents/constitution/constitution-republic-south-africa-04-feb-1997) | Government-hosted Constitution information | Not a complete searchable database of all current law. |
| [Department of Justice — Legislation](https://www.justice.gov.za/legislation/legislation_main.htm) | Acts, bills, subordinate legislation, rules and practice directions | Some listings have cut-off dates; check later amendments and Gazette publications. |
| [SAFLII — Legislation](https://www.saflii.org/content/legislation.html) | Public access to legislation and gazettes | View the site under its terms. Do not scrape, bulk-download, index, embed, use for RAG, train on, or reuse content in automated legal research without the written permission required by SAFLII's current terms. |
| [SAFLII — Judgments](https://www.saflii.org/) | Public access to judgments and legal information | Public access is not a general licence for AI or bulk reuse. Review the current terms and obtain written permission where required. |
| [Constitutional Court collection](https://collections.concourt.org.za/) | Official Constitutional Court judgments and collection records | Check item-level terms, citation, version and publication information before reuse. |

## Rules for future source connectors

1. Store jurisdiction, court/issuer, citation or Act identifier, source URL, publication date, effective/commencement date where available, retrieved date, version, paragraph/section and reuse permission.
2. Keep original source excerpts separate from learner-provided notes and AI-generated explanations.
3. Link back to the original source and display missing amendment history or uncertainty.
4. Never label a source “current” without a defined and monitored update process.
5. Do not crawl, bulk-download, index, republish, embed or train on content until its licence and terms explicitly permit the intended use or written permission has been obtained.
6. Keep each jurisdiction separate in storage, retrieval, prompts and evaluation.
7. A source connector must include provenance logs and a process to remove or correct material when permission changes or an error is found.
